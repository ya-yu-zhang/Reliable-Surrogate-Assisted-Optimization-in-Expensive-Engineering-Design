Local Response Stability for Certified Two-Step Rollout in Engineering Optimization

Two-step rollout selects a first engineering design by anticipating a response-dependent continuation. A finite-menu algorithm is developed in which response-stability regions are certified and conditional posteriors are constructed for encountered actions. Regional terminal-reward bounds enclose the contribution of uncomputed regions and certify first-action selection to a prescribed model-value tolerance. The certificate covers positive completions of uncomputed conditional laws, and a piecewise error bound separates policy switches from smooth integration. Code and clean training inputs were frozen before one paired evaluation on 43 previously observed beam-design states. The designs satisfy a prescribed structural volume constraint. Both regional and uniform-reward routes certify all states without complete continuation projection. Regional bounds reduce cell operations from 640 to 585 and conditional posterior constructions from 161 to 157. Checked execution takes 12.23 and 10.73 seconds, respectively, plus shared preparation. Certified decision computation is established for a finite structural-design problem; previously paid physical outcomes remain mixed.

Keywords: surrogate-assisted engineering optimization; two-step rollout; Gaussian processes; response stability; validated numerical integration
