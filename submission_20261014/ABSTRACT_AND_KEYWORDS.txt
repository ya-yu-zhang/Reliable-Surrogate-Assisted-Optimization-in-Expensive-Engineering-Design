Local Response Stability for Certified Two-Step Rollout in Engineering Optimization

Two-step rollout couples first-action selection to response-dependent continuation. We propose a finite-menu algorithm that discovers certified stability regions and requests conditional posterior calculations only for encountered actions. Regional terminal-reward enclosures certify selection without a complete continuation partition. The guarantee covers every positive completion of uncomputed conditional responses, including discontinuous fixed-policy rewards. Code and clean training inputs were frozen before one paired evaluation on 43 archived beam-design states. Both regional and uniform-reward routes certify all states, matching earlier selections without reading historical action domains. Regional bounds reduce cell operations from 640 to 585 and conditional posterior constructions from 161 to 157. Their checked stage takes 12.23 seconds versus 10.73 seconds, with shared preparation charged separately. Existing engineering outcomes remain mixed. The contribution is a response-stability-guided algorithm for certified rollout selection under a declared conditional model. Numerical applicability is demonstrated; engineering-policy superiority, acceleration and continuous-domain convergence remain unestablished.

Keywords: surrogate-assisted engineering optimization; two-step rollout; Gaussian processes; response stability; validated numerical integration
