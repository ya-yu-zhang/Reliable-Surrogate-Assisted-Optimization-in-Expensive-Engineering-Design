Certified Action Sets and Decision Limits in Surrogate-Assisted Engineering Optimization

We study certified numerical computation of possible next actions before a pending engineering response is observed. Classical affine-envelope geometry is adapted to fixed-kernel Gaussian-process coefficient bands, closed response intervals and independently checked exclusions. A common-scale integer implementation agrees exactly with an optimized pairwise control on all 228 archived beam-design branch/domain cases. Filtering is 2.71 times faster; including coefficient reconstruction and independent checking gives a 1.36-fold improvement. Against the same-complexity Fraction envelope, the corresponding ratios are 4.46 and 1.70. A frozen nominal-versus-band diagnostic finds identical retained indices in every case, although all 908 shared intervals widen. Thus these engineering data do not demonstrate rescue of additional actions. An elementary domain-cover identity explains the failed H1 lower certificates and permits their scalar rejection without action-set construction in all 142 nonbaseline requests. Two cases selected before diagnostic outputs illustrate the required information and cost boundaries. These dependent development results establish a specialized implementation and its decision limits, rather than a new optimization principle, improved physical designs or deployment savings.

Keywords: surrogate-assisted engineering optimization; Gaussian processes; certified action sets; exact arithmetic; certificate routing
