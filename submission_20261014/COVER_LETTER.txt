Dear Editors,

This manuscript proposes a finite-menu rollout selection algorithm that discovers certified response-stability regions and requests conditional posterior calculations only for encountered continuations. A positive terminal-reward remainder bounds uncomputed regions, and a checked inequality certifies the first action for every admissible positive completion of the uncomputed conditional laws. A discontinuity example and a local switching/integration error bound specify its mathematical scope. Existing rollout, EI decompositions, affine envelopes and epsilon-GP planning are acknowledged as established tools.

Code, protocol and 43 clean beam-training snapshots were committed before one paired development run. Both regional and uniform-reward routes certify all states without historical action domains, old posteriors or complete response projection. Regional bounds reduce cell operations from 640 to 585 and conditional posterior constructions from 161 to 157. They take 12.23 seconds versus 10.73 seconds for the checked stage, with 2.72 seconds shared preparation charged separately. No time advantage is claimed. Existing engineering outcomes remain mixed.

The contribution is a computational method for a declared engineering optimization subproblem.
