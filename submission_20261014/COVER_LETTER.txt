Relation to the journal's aims and scope
The manuscript concerns numerical optimization of a first engineering design under a response-dependent continuation. Local action-identity certification, conditional-posterior queries and reward integration are coupled through a checked regret stopping rule. The optimization step is applied to fixed-volume MBB beam compliance, where the first action specifies the next feasible structural design for evaluation. This connects an algorithmic development in finite-menu optimization to engineering decision support.

Statement of contribution (78 words)
A finite-menu rollout algorithm is developed for engineering design decisions under a response-dependent continuation. Local action-identity certification, conditional-posterior queries and reward integration are jointly scheduled using regional terminal-reward bounds. First-action selection is certified to a prescribed model-value tolerance before the continuation partition is complete. The guarantee covers admissible positive completions, with policy-switch and smooth-integration errors treated separately. In fixed-volume beam compliance decisions, both evaluated routes certify all 43 archived states; regional bounds reduce cell operations and conditional-posterior requests.

The paired calculation uses frozen code and 43 clean training snapshots. Regional and uniform-reward routes certify all states without complete continuation projection. Regional bounds reduce cell operations from 640 to 585 and conditional-posterior constructions from 161 to 157. Checked execution takes 12.23 s and 10.73 s, respectively, plus shared preparation. Previously paid two-evaluation physical outcomes remain mixed.
