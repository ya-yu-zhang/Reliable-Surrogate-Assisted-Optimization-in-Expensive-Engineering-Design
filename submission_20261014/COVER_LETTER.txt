Re: Local Response Stability for Certified Two-Step Rollout in Engineering Optimization

Dear Editors,

This manuscript presents a finite-menu two-step rollout solver for positive engineering responses. A certified response-stability region and bounded terminal reward control the error from fixing the continuation locally. Competitive branches receive complete projection only when needed. Posterior residual enclosures, directed integration and a fresh certificate reader support the declared first-action selection tolerance.

The contribution is the response-stability mechanism and checked numerical solution of this specific optimization subproblem. Existing rollout, finite policy menus, epsilon-optimal GP planning, affine envelopes and log-objective EI are acknowledged. No claim of generic priority or continuous-domain convergence is made.

Code, protocol and 43 archived beam-state input snapshots were saved before the single paired execution. Both methods pass on all states with matching choices. Complete projections decrease from 114 to 25; however, the local checked stage takes 5.47 seconds versus 4.93 seconds for the direct route, with common preparation reported separately. There is no timing-advantage claim. Already paid two-evaluation engineering replay is mixed and does not establish policy superiority.

The new v2 numerical record is complete and distinct from an older unavailable positive-scope execution. Historical raw-Gaussian negative results and the three-file paired-grid raw-data gap remain disclosed. No new physical evaluation, retry, replacement or independent optimization sample was acquired.
