Re: Local Response Stability for Certified Two-Step Rollout in Engineering Optimization

Dear Editors,

The manuscript presents a finite-menu rollout algorithm in which the first engineering design is optimized under a prescribed response-dependent continuation. Local response-stability certification, conditional-posterior construction and reward integration are scheduled through a common value-uncertainty calculation. Uncomputed contributions are enclosed by regional terminal-reward bounds, allowing the first-action maximization to stop at a checked model-value tolerance before the continuation partition is complete. The selection guarantee covers positive completions of uncomputed conditional laws, and policy-switch error is separated from smooth integration error.

The engineering application uses a fixed-volume MBB beam workflow. Code and 43 clean training snapshots were frozen before one paired comparison. Both routes certify all states without complete continuation projection. Regional bounds reduce cell operations from 640 to 585 and conditional posterior constructions from 161 to 157; checked execution takes 12.23 s and 10.73 s, respectively, plus shared preparation. Previously paid two-evaluation outcomes provide the engineering comparison and remain mixed. The paper contributes a certified computational method for the specified engineering decision problem.

The contribution addresses the journal's focus on numerical optimization and engineering decision support: a checked stopping rule and selective conditional computation are coupled to the next feasible structural design selected for compliance evaluation. The method is compared conceptually with established two-step optimization, rollout, affine-envelope and epsilon-GP planning. The supplement supplies proofs, numerical evidence, earlier engineering findings and source-availability information. The private preparation branch preserves code, inputs and complete current numerical records. Known AI assistance is disclosed.
