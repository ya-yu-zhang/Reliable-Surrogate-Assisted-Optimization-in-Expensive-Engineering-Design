# Measured paired-grid evidence and limits

Selection was frozen before new grid labels: seven distinct archived
immediate-to-later changes, one independent EI-continuation immediate-proxy
mismatch, four unchanged different-first-action controls and two structural
aliases. These 14 dependent contrasts contain 104 distinct design points from
nine existing states. They are selected numerical checks, not fresh optimization
replications or an estimate of population ranking reliability.

The contexts are 100x50, 150x75 and 200x100 grids, each under native parameter
resampling and conservative cell-average transfer of the 100x50 density field.
Volume fraction, normalized supports, force location and material settings are
checked. Responses are reused only within an identical mesh/density context.
The two mappings coincide on the base grid; their finer fields can differ.

## Directly recorded results

* All 14 fixed h5 classifications persist across all six contexts. Eight endpoint
  pairs differ and all eight preserve classification; six pairs have the same
  endpoint, so their equality is structural and does not supply eight-plus-six
  independent ranking tests. S01/S02/S07 are better; S03--S06/S08 worse; S09--S14 tie.
* The minimum among retained branch candidates gives no h5 classification change
  in these selected cases. This secondary target reselects among recorded points,
  not among all original training points or newly optimized finer-grid designs.
* One of 104 coarse design points exceeds the unchanged 1e-8 relative agreement
  tolerance (absolute allowance 1e-8): archived compliance 60220706.07686931,
  remeasured compliance 60220701.98290202, relative discrepancy
  6.798271816186852e-8. This is a high-compliance branch candidate, not the best
  endpoint. The original protocol gate remains failed.
* Maximum recorded free-equilibrium relative residual is 6.185270531163674e-7;
  no response exceeds the predeclared 1e-6 residual flag. Maximum relative
  energy/work discrepancy is 3.5447876895864194e-7. These algebraic checks do not
  certify discretization accuracy.
* Absolute objective variation relative to the rechecked base-grid values reaches
  26.5739% under native 200x100 resampling and 32.0590% under conservative transfer.
  These are observed mesh/mapping differences, not established solver errors.

## Failure, recovery and claim boundary

The initial run stopped on its fourth attempt at the original source-agreement
assertion, before retaining that attempt's response/time. The recovery amendment
records agreement flags after retaining each label instead of aborting further
selected sensitivity checks. It changes neither the tolerance nor selected
points. Three original successful responses are reused; the missing response is
remeasured. Four earlier plus 517 recovery attempts give 521 paid physics attempts.
The original failed gate is not converted into a passed protocol result.

Ranking stability in these selected fixed-design pairs does not establish mesh
convergence, a physical error bound for the 0.1% decision margin, or an industrial
deployment benefit. No fine-grid GP fit or optimizer run was performed and all
original training points were not refined. Keep fixed-discrete-problem method
claims separate from physical accuracy claims. The complete revision paid-call
ledger is now 55,305, including all previously disclosed lost/recovery attempts.
