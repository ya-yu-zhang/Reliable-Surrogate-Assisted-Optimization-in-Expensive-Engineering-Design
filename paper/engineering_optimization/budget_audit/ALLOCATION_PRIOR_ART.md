# Allocation prior-art check for authors

Source checked12 October2026:
Li, Jamieson, DeSalvo, Rostamizadeh and Talwalkar, Hyperband: A Novel Bandit-Based
Approach to Hyperparameter Optimization, JMLR18(185):1--52,2018.
Primary journal abstract: https://jmlr.org/papers/v18/16-558.html

The abstract explicitly describes adaptive resource allocation and early stopping
for sampled hyperparameter configurations. Therefore, staged budgets, screening
and early stopping cannot by themselves be claimed as this project's novelty.
This record is limited to the verified abstract; it is not a complete prior-art
review or an experimental implementation/comparison with Hyperband.

Existing Engineering Optimization diagnostic benchmarking comparison:
https://doi.org/10.1080/0305215X.2024.2381818
That work includes effectiveness, efficiency, reliability, parameter controllability
and runtime dynamics. It must not be characterized as endpoint-only benchmarking.

For independent author assessment: this repository's evaluated object is a
first-action intervention at a fixed learned state, with exact action/response
equivalence, branch-specific information boundaries and conditional finite-horizon
classification. The new allocator is a tested heuristic inside that evidence
contract; neither absence of prior art nor an optimizer improvement is certified.
